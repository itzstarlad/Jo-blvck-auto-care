Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about our auto care services. If you need more information, contact us directly.

Are you certified mechanics?

Yes. We are certified Mechanical Engineers - we went to school and got certified, plus we have years of real practical, hands-on workshop experience. We combine academic knowledge with field skill.

What services does JO-BLVCK AUTO CARE offer?

We offer engine diagnostics and repairs, oil changes and fluid checks, timing belt replacement, brake system repairs, general vehicle check-ups and other auto repairs.

Do you repair different types of vehicles?

Yes. While we are specialists in Japanese car models such as Toyota, Honda, Lexus and other Japanese brands, we also professionally repair other vehicle types including Mercedes-Benz, SUVs and pickup trucks like Hilux. Our certified Mechanical Engineering background allows us to diagnose and repair diverse models with precision.

Where is your workshop located?

We are located around the Federal University of Technology Ilaro, Ogun State, Nigeria. Please contact us for directions before visiting.

How can I book a repair or inspection?

You can book easily by: 1) Clicking 'Schedule a Service' on the website, 2) Calling us directly on 0703 524 9778, or 3) Chatting with us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your time and vehicle details immediately.

Do I need to book before visiting?

While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend booking in advance to reduce wait time and ensure we dedicate quality time to your vehicle. Booking guarantees prompt attention from our certified engineers.

Can I ask for a price before bringing my vehicle?

Yes. Tell us about your vehicle and the problem. We can discuss likely costs, although the final price may depend on inspection and any parts required.

How long will my vehicle repair take?

Repair time depends on the issue. Minor services like oil change or diagnostics can be same-day. Major repairs may take longer. After inspection, we give you a clear timeline and keep you updated throughout.

Do you use genuine spare parts?

Yes. We use quality, genuine or recommended quality parts to ensure durability and safety. We discuss part options and costs with you transparently before replacement.

Do you offer warranty on repairs?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship. Because we are certified and use quality parts, we offer service assurance on our repairs. We are committed to customer satisfaction.

What should I do if my car develops a serious problem on the road?

If possible, stop somewhere safe. If you suspect overheating or another serious fault, switch off the engine and contact us for guidance. Do not continue driving if it may be unsafe.
